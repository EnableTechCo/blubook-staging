# Responsive behavior

## 1440px+

- 256px labelled navigation rail with a floating glass treatment.
- Sticky account toolbar and multi-column dashboard grids.
- Operational tables retain horizontal comparison behavior and sticky columns.

## 1024px

- The labelled rail remains visible so navigation is never icon-only.
- Content grids reduce columns without changing information order.

## 768px

- The labelled rail remains usable at 232px; main content wraps and dense tables scroll inside
  their own regions.
- Forms and card grids use their existing tablet breakpoints.

## 390px

- Existing mobile menu behavior is preserved in the sticky toolbar.
- Cards and forms stack in source order; actions wrap; tables remain internally scrollable.
- Controls keep a minimum 44px target and important data/actions are not hidden.

## Accessibility

- Existing semantic landmarks, labels, focus order, dialogs, table semantics, and status text stay
  intact.
- Focus rings remain high contrast against white and blue-grey surfaces.
- Reduced-motion preferences remove nonessential transition duration and smooth scrolling.
